# Surrealism — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/surreal-gradient-ui, https://www.awwwards.com/

## Observed Design Principles
Dreamlike spatial depth, fluid mesh gradients, floating organic forms, and unexpected luminous juxtapositions.

## Light Variant Specification
Iridescent pearl `#FAF7FF` canvas, violet `#7C3AED` + peach `#FB7185` mesh accents, `#1E1B4B` deep indigo ink.

## Dark Variant Specification
Twilight `#090617` void, luminous orchid `#C084FC` + aurora teal `#2DD4BF`, `#F5F3FF` ink.

## Typography
Editorial display serif paired with airy sans, relaxed tracking.

## Colour
Dreamlike pastel-to-luminous mesh gradients anchored by deep indigo ink.

## Geometry
22px–32px organic fluid radii, delicate 1px translucent borders.

## Surface & Texture
Soft ambient glows, subtle grain over mesh gradients, floating elevation.

## Motion & Interaction
360ms slow floating cubic-bezier `(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` ease.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons float with ambient aura shadows; cards use soft translucent gradient backdrops.

## What to Avoid
Rigid 0px industrial boxes or harsh mechanical snapping.
