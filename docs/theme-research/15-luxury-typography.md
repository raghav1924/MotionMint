# Luxury Typography — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://www.awwwards.com/, https://www.siteinspire.com/websites

## Observed Design Principles
High-contrast serif display headings, generous letter-spacing, champagne/gold accents, and understated editorial restraint.

## Light Variant Specification
Alabaster `#FAF8F5` canvas, `#FFFFFF` surface, `#141210` obsidian ink, `#A68446` champagne gold accent.

## Dark Variant Specification
Obsidian `#0C0B0A` canvas, `#151311` velvet surface, `#F5F0E6` ivory ink, `#D4AF37` gold accent.

## Typography
High-contrast Didone/serif display + refined sans body.

## Colour
Alabaster/obsidian neutrals with restrained metallic gold.

## Geometry
2px–6px refined corners, 1px hairline borders.

## Surface & Texture
Matte luxury planes with subtle gold border highlights.

## Motion & Interaction
300ms theatrical slow ease.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons expand letter-spacing subtly on hover; cards use refined serif headings.

## What to Avoid
Loud neon colors, chunky comic borders, or dense clutter.
