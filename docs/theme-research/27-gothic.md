# Gothic — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://www.pinterest.com/, https://www.awwwards.com/

## Observed Design Principles
Dramatic dark cathedral atmosphere, arched framing cues, tarnished silver & crimson accents, and solemn serif authority.

## Light Variant Specification
Cathedral stone `#EFECE8` canvas, `#FAF8F5` card, `#181416` ink, `#991B1B` crimson + `#52525B` pewter silver accents.

## Dark Variant Specification
Obsidian nave `#09080A` canvas, `#131116` stone card, `#ECE8E4` ivory ink, `#DC2626` crimson + `#A1A1AA` silver accents.

## Typography
Dramatic high-contrast serif headings + crisp sans body.

## Colour
Obsidian, stone, pewter silver, and deep crimson.

## Geometry
4px–12px pointed/arched framing, 1.5px silver/stone borders.

## Surface & Texture
Deep velvet shadows and subtle stone contrast.

## Motion & Interaction
280ms solemn measured transitions.

## Translation to React Components
Cards frame content with dramatic contrast and silver/crimson highlights.

## What to Avoid
Playful clay bubbles or bright sunshine yellows.
