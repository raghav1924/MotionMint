# Claymorphism — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/claymorphism, https://www.behance.net/

## Observed Design Principles
Puffy 3D plasticine forms, generous 24px+ rounded corners, and a 3-layer inner+outer shadow recipe.

## Light Variant Specification
Soft lavender-white `#F3F0FF` canvas, `#FFFFFF` clay surface, `#1E1B4B` ink, `#7C3AED` accent, inflated inner+outer shadows.

## Dark Variant Specification
Deep plum-slate `#141122` canvas, `#211D35` clay surface, `#F5F3FF` ink, `#A78BFA` accent, dark clay dome highlights.

## Typography
Friendly rounded geometric sans, 700 weight.

## Colour
Playful pastel-to-rich clay tones with high-contrast text.

## Geometry
20px–28px chunky pillow radii.

## Surface & Texture
Stacked outer drop shadow + top light inset + bottom shade inset.

## Motion & Interaction
220ms squishy spring (`stiffness: 340, damping: 16`).

## Translation to React Components
Buttons squish softly on press; cards look puffed and tactile.

## What to Avoid
0px sharp corners or flat 1px wireframes.
