# Cybercore — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/cybercore-ui, https://www.pinterest.com/

## Observed Design Principles
Green phosphor CRT displays, dense technical telemetry, terminal-grid precision, and crisp monospace readouts.

## Light Variant Specification
Phosphor lab paper `#ECFDF5` canvas, `#FFFFFF` terminal card, `#064E3B` deep forest ink, `#059669` phosphor accent.

## Dark Variant Specification
CRT black `#030A06` canvas, `#07140D` terminal surface, `#A7F3D0` phosphor ink, `#22C55E` + `#4ADE80` terminal glow.

## Typography
JetBrains Mono throughout, uppercase telemetry tags.

## Colour
Phosphor emerald/green on CRT black or lab paper.

## Geometry
2px–4px crisp terminal corners, 1px grid borders.

## Surface & Texture
Subtle CRT scanline texture and phosphor glow.

## Motion & Interaction
100ms crisp terminal blink/snap.

## Translation to React Components
Controls read like live telemetry modules with monospace precision.

## What to Avoid
Warm bohemian tones or soft clay pillows.
