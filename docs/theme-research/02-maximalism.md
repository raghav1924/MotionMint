# Maximalism — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/maximalism-web-design, https://www.awwwards.com/

## Observed Design Principles
Controlled chaos, dense visual storytelling, overlapping sticker badges, loud saturated palettes, and kinetic display typography.

## Light Variant Specification
Warm cream `#FFFDF5` canvas, saturated magenta `#E11D48` + cobalt `#2563EB` + acid `#CA8A04`, `#18181B` ink, thick 2px borders.

## Dark Variant Specification
Deep velvet `#12091B` canvas, electric coral `#FB7185` + cyan `#22D3EE` + acid lime `#DFFF00`, `#FAFAFA` ink.

## Typography
Display serif + geometric grotesque pairing, tight `-0.03em` tracking, bold 800 weight.

## Colour
High-saturation multi-accent palette anchored by a strict contrast foreground.

## Geometry
14px–20px mixed radii, 2px–3px bold borders, layered badge chips.

## Surface & Texture
Multi-layer offset shadows, subtle halftone/grain texture, saturated accent pills.

## Motion & Interaction
240ms spring overshoot (`stiffness: 380, damping: 18`) with playful micro-rotation.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons carry bold saturated fills and stacked border depth; cards layer header badges and accent strips.

## What to Avoid
Unreadable low-contrast text over busy textures or broken keyboard focus states.
