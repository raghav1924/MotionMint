# Anthropomorphic — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/character-ui, https://www.awwwards.com/

## Observed Design Principles
Friendly expressive character personality, bouncy spring reactions, pill/bubble geometry, and playful tactile feedback.

## Light Variant Specification
Cheerful cream `#FFFDF7` canvas, `#FFFFFF` bubble card, `#1E293B` ink, `#F97316` tangerine + `#0EA5E9` sky accents.

## Dark Variant Specification
Cozy night `#141824` canvas, `#1E2436` bubble card, `#F8FAFC` ink, `#FB923C` + `#38BDF8` friendly accents.

## Typography
Friendly rounded geometric sans, 700 weight.

## Colour
Warm optimistic primaries with friendly contrast.

## Geometry
18px–24px bubbly radii, 2px friendly borders.

## Surface & Texture
Playful soft shadows and expressive hover lift.

## Motion & Interaction
240ms bouncy spring (`stiffness: 420, damping: 16`).

## Translation to React Components
Buttons bounce playfully on tap; cards feel approachable and alive.

## What to Avoid
Cold brutalist rigidity or harsh 0px edges.
