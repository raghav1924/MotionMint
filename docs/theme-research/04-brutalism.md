# Brutalism — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://www.awwwards.com/, https://www.behance.net/

## Observed Design Principles
Raw structural honesty, unpolished monospace details, thick 2px–3px solid borders, zero radii, and high-contrast monochrome.

## Light Variant Specification
Raw concrete `#E5E5E0` canvas, `#000000` ink, `#0000FF` raw hyperlink blue accent, 2px solid `#000000` borders.

## Dark Variant Specification
Asphalt `#0A0A0A` canvas, `#F5F5F0` ink, `#38BDF8` / `#FACC15` stark industrial accent, 2px `#F5F5F0` borders.

## Typography
JetBrains Mono / system monospace, raw uppercase tags, 700 weight.

## Colour
Concrete gray, pure black/white, and raw primary utility accents.

## Geometry
0px sharp corners, 2px–3px uniform borders.

## Surface & Texture
Unsoftened flat planes with raw inverted active states.

## Motion & Interaction
Instant 60ms–90ms mechanical state flips.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons invert background/foreground immediately on hover/active; inputs look like raw terminal boxes.

## What to Avoid
Soft rounded pills, pastel blurs, or ornamental flourishes.
