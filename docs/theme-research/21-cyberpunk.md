# Cyberpunk — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/cyberpunk-ui, https://www.behance.net/

## Observed Design Principles
Neon cyan/magenta/electric yellow over industrial surfaces, angled technical geometry, and HUD status readouts.

## Light Variant Specification
High-visibility industrial lab `#F0F4F8` with `#0F172A` ink, `#0284C7` cyan + `#DB2777` magenta + `#CA8A04` hazard accents.

## Dark Variant Specification
Night City `#07070D` canvas, `#11111B` HUD panel, `#F8FAFC` ink, `#00F0FF` neon cyan + `#FF007F` magenta + `#FACC15` yellow.

## Typography
Monospace HUD labels + sharp geometric display.

## Colour
Neon cyan, hot magenta, hazard yellow, and industrial charcoal.

## Geometry
2px–4px angled/chamfered tech corners, 1.5px neon borders.

## Surface & Texture
Subtle neon border glow and dark industrial paneling.

## Motion & Interaction
120ms sharp HUD snap.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons and inputs glow along edges on focus/hover; cards look like tactical HUD modules.

## What to Avoid
Warm pastel clay or ornate Victorian curls.
