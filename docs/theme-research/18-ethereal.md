# Ethereal — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/surreal-gradient-ui, https://www.awwwards.com/

## Observed Design Principles
Atmospheric lighting, soft mist gradients, translucent halo borders, and weightless spatial calm.

## Light Variant Specification
Mist `#F7F9FC` canvas, `rgba(255,255,255,0.85)` surface, `#1E293B` ink, `#6366F1` + `#38BDF8` soft aura.

## Dark Variant Specification
Starlight `#070A12` canvas, `#101624` mist surface, `#F1F5F9` ink, `#818CF8` + `#67E8F9` soft aura.

## Typography
Airy light-to-regular sans-serif with open tracking.

## Colour
Soft atmospheric blues, lavenders, and pearl whites.

## Geometry
20px–26px soft rounded forms.

## Surface & Texture
Soft diffused halo shadows and subtle blur.

## Motion & Interaction
320ms weightless float.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons glow softly on focus/hover; cards feel light and suspended.

## What to Avoid
Hard 0px black borders or aggressive visual noise.
