# Y2K — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/y2k-ui, https://www.pinterest.com/

## Observed Design Principles
Turn-of-the-millennium liquid chrome, translucent aqua/silver bevels, starbursts, and glossy futuristic highlights.

## Light Variant Specification
Silver ice `#EEF2F7` canvas, metallic chrome `#FFFFFF`–`#DCE4F0` surface, `#0F172A` ink, `#EC4899` + `#06B6D4` Y2K accents.

## Dark Variant Specification
Chrome night `#0D111A` canvas, brushed `#192130` metallic surface, `#F8FAFC` ink, `#F472B6` + `#22D3EE` glossy accents.

## Typography
Futuristic geometric sans + crisp mono tech tags.

## Colour
Metallic silver, ice blue, hot pink, and acid cyan.

## Geometry
14px–999px pill/capsule shapes, glossy 1.5px metallic borders.

## Surface & Texture
Subtle metallic linear gradients and glossy top highlights.

## Motion & Interaction
180ms crisp futuristic glide.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons wear metallic capsule sheen; cards feature chrome border highlights.

## What to Avoid
Dry flat paper textures or muted earth tones.
