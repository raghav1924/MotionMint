# Minimalism — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/minimalism-dashboard, https://www.siteinspire.com/websites

## Observed Design Principles
Radical reduction, functional negative space, 3-step type scale, restrained 60-30-10 neutral hierarchy, and borderless or hairline separation.

## Light Variant Specification
Crisp `#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC` canvas, `#111827` charcoal ink, `#64748B` muted slate, 1px `#E2E8F0` hairlines, subtle `#4F46E5` or `#0F172A` focus.

## Dark Variant Specification
Matte `#09090B` canvas, `#121215` elevated surface, `#F4F4F5` soft white text, `#71717A` secondary, `#27272A` hairlines, restrained `#E4E4E7` accent.

## Typography
Inter / Geist sans-serif, tabular numerals, `-0.02em` tracking, 500–600 headings.

## Colour
Monochromatic neutral scale with a single disciplined accent for primary CTAs.

## Geometry
8px–12px clean radii, 1px hairline borders, generous inner padding.

## Surface & Texture
Flat or 1px-bordered surfaces with whisper-quiet `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)` elevation.

## Motion & Interaction
150ms cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1) micro-transitions; zero bounce.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons use crisp hairline outlines and subtle hover fills; inputs stay border-bottom or 1px minimal; cards rely on whitespace.

## What to Avoid
Pure `#000000` harsh contrast, heavy drop shadows, decorative gradients, or multi-color noise.
