# Swiss Design — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/swiss-style-web, https://www.siteinspire.com/websites

## Observed Design Principles
International Typographic Style: mathematical 8px/12-col grids, exposed 1px structural rules, flush-left hierarchy, and objective clarity.

## Light Variant Specification
Off-white `#F4F4F0` archival paper, pitch `#111111` ink, signal red `#FF3300` accent, 1px `#111111` / `rgba(0,0,0,0.18)` grid rules.

## Dark Variant Specification
Graphite `#111111` field, `#F4F4F0` crisp ink, signal red `#FF4411` accent, `rgba(255,255,255,0.22)` exposed grid lines.

## Typography
Neo-grotesque sans (Helvetica Neue / Inter), uppercase metadata labels, `0px`–`2px` sharp alignment.

## Colour
Strict monochrome + single signal red accent.

## Geometry
0px–2px sharp rectangular corners, 1px exposed structural borders.

## Surface & Texture
Zero drop shadows; depth expressed strictly through alignment and rules.

## Motion & Interaction
140ms crisp linear/expo snaps with zero overshoot.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons are sharp rectangular blocks that invert cleanly on hover; inputs use stark 1px rules; cards expose internal dividers.

## What to Avoid
Rounded pill clutter, soft blurs, centered decorative prose, or gradients.
