# Editorial Design — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://www.siteinspire.com/websites, https://www.awwwards.com/

## Observed Design Principles
Magazine broadsheet composition, rule dividers, serif headlines paired with crisp metadata footnotes, and airy margins.

## Light Variant Specification
Newsprint `#FBFBF9` canvas, `#FFFFFF` column, `#111111` ink, `#B91C1C` editorial red accent.

## Dark Variant Specification
Darkroom `#111113` canvas, `#18181B` column, `#F4F4F0` ink, `#F87171` editorial red accent.

## Typography
Classic editorial serif headings + crisp sans body + mono footnotes.

## Colour
Ink, paper, and restrained editorial crimson.

## Geometry
4px–6px clean print corners, 1px column rules.

## Surface & Texture
Flat print-like surfaces separated by crisp rules.

## Motion & Interaction
200ms calm editorial ease.

## Translation to React Components
Cards read like structured magazine callouts with serif titles and hairline dividers.

## What to Avoid
Neon glows or bouncy toy physics.
