# Scrapbook — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/scrapbook-collage-ui, https://www.pinterest.com/

## Observed Design Principles
Layered paper cutouts, dashed masking-tape labels, subtle tilt (`-0.6deg`), warm archival tones, and tactile sticker badges.

## Light Variant Specification
Warm kraft/paper `#FAF5EB` canvas, `#FFFFFF` index card, `#292524` ink, terracotta `#EA580C` + sage `#16A34A` tape accents, dashed `#D6CBB8` borders.

## Dark Variant Specification
Dark archival board `#191614` canvas, `#24201C` dark paper card, `#F5EFE6` cream ink, amber `#F59E0B` + coral `#FB7185` labels, dashed `#443D37` borders.

## Typography
Editorial serif + typewriter monospace labels.

## Colour
Warm paper, kraft, ink black, and physical stationery accents.

## Geometry
4px–10px paper corners, dashed/stitched borders.

## Surface & Texture
Paper-cutout drop shadow (`2px 4px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`), subtle paper grain.

## Motion & Interaction
200ms tactile paper-lift with slight `-0.5deg` hover tilt.

## Translation to React Components
Cards feature dashed borders and paper-tag badges; buttons look like stamped labels.

## What to Avoid
Sterile glass blur or cold cyberpunk neons.
