# Victorian — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://www.pinterest.com/, https://www.behance.net/

## Observed Design Principles
Ornamental engraved framing, classic serif typography, deep parlor tones (emerald/oxblood/antique gold), and bookish symmetry.

## Light Variant Specification
Aged parchment `#F5EFE2` canvas, `#FCF9F2` card, `#231B15` sepia-black ink, `#7C2D12` oxblood + `#B45309` antique gold accents.

## Dark Variant Specification
Velvet parlor `#140E0E` canvas, `#1E1616` engraved card, `#F3EAD8` parchment ink, `#D4AF37` gold + `#B91C1C` crimson accents.

## Typography
Classic ornate serif (`Playfair Display` / Georgia) with tracked uppercase subtitles.

## Colour
Parchment, oxblood, forest, and antique gold.

## Geometry
4px–6px framed corners, double/ornamental borders.

## Surface & Texture
Rich matte material with classic engraved inset border feel.

## Motion & Interaction
280ms stately theatrical ease.

## Translation to React Components
Cards frame content like archival plates; buttons carry classic serif elegance.

## What to Avoid
Y2K chrome, neon cyan, or pill-shaped plastic.
