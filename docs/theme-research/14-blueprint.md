# Conceptual Sketch / Blueprint — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/blueprint-ui, https://www.behance.net/

## Observed Design Principles
Technical drafting gridlines, CAD callouts, monospace coordinates, and crisp hairline crosshairs.

## Light Variant Specification
Pale drafting paper `#F0F6FF` canvas, `#FFFFFF` sheet, `#0F2942` navy ink, `#0284C7` cyan accent, `rgba(2,132,199,0.16)` gridlines.

## Dark Variant Specification
Deep blueprint navy `#07162B` field, `#0B203D` schematic layer, `#E0F2FE` crisp ink, `#38BDF8` cyan callouts, `rgba(56,189,248,0.18)` gridlines.

## Typography
JetBrains Mono technical typography, uppercase drafting annotations.

## Colour
Drafting paper / deep blueprint navy + cyan technical callouts.

## Geometry
2px–4px technical corners, 1px precision lines.

## Surface & Texture
Exposed background coordinate grid (`20px × 20px`) with crisp hairline borders.

## Motion & Interaction
150ms precision linear/cad snaps.

## Translation to React Components
Cards display subtle gridlines and technical labels; controls feel like CAD instruments.

## What to Avoid
Organic blobs, heavy drop shadows, or serif ornaments.
