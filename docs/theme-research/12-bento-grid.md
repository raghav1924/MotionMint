# Bento Grid — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/bento-grid, https://www.siteinspire.com/websites

## Observed Design Principles
Modular asymmetric cell partitioning, crisp neutral cards, structured hierarchy, and Apple-inspired hardware clarity.

## Light Variant Specification
Cool `#F4F4F6` tray, `#FFFFFF` cells, `#09090B` ink, `#059669` emerald accent, `1px` `rgba(9,9,11,0.08)` borders.

## Dark Variant Specification
Matte `#0B0B0E` tray, `#141418` cells, `#FAFAFA` ink, `#10B981` emerald accent, `1px` `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)` borders.

## Typography
Inter / Geist geometric sans, `-0.02em` tight tracking.

## Colour
Clean neutral surfaces with crisp emerald/blue functional accents.

## Geometry
18px–24px modular cell radii.

## Surface & Texture
Subtle elevation (`0 2px 10px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`) with clean inner cell dividers.

## Motion & Interaction
180ms smooth cubic-bezier `(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`.

## Translation to React Components
Cards organize content into clean modular cells with consistent internal gutters.

## What to Avoid
Irregular tilted stickers or noisy textures.
