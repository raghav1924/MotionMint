# Graffiti / Streetwear — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/streetwear-web-design, https://www.behance.net/

## Observed Design Principles
Stencil display energy, wheatpaste poster contrast, bold marker accents, and gritty urban drop depth.

## Light Variant Specification
Concrete poster `#F3F4F6` canvas, `#FFFFFF` sticker card, `#111827` ink, `#EA580C` spray orange + `#84CC16` acid lime.

## Dark Variant Specification
Alleyway `#0D0E11` canvas, `#17191F` concrete card, `#F9FAFB` ink, `#FB923C` spray orange + `#DFFF00` acid lime.

## Typography
Heavy condensed display + stencil/mono tags.

## Colour
Concrete neutrals slashed with spray-paint orange and acid lime.

## Geometry
4px–8px poster corners, 2px bold stencil borders.

## Surface & Texture
Hard urban drop shadow + subtle distressed texture.

## Motion & Interaction
130ms punchy street snap.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons feel like bold street stickers; cards have high-contrast poster energy.

## What to Avoid
Delicate pastel ethereality or classical Victorian ornament.
