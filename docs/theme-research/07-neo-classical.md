# Neo-classical — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://www.siteinspire.com/websites, https://www.behance.net/

## Observed Design Principles
Architectural symmetry, high-contrast serif headlines, travertine/marble palettes, and double-rule classic framing.

## Light Variant Specification
Warm limestone `#F6F3EC` canvas, `#1C1917` charcoal ink, antique bronze `#9A7B4F` accent, `#D6CFC2` stone borders.

## Dark Variant Specification
Basalt `#121110` canvas, `#F3EFE6` ivory ink, brushed gold `#D4AF37` accent, `#2E2B26` borders.

## Typography
High-contrast serif (`Didot` / `Playfair Display` / Georgia) with generous letter-spacing.

## Colour
Limestone, ivory, charcoal, and restrained bronze/gold.

## Geometry
2px–4px architectural corners, crisp 1px classical rules.

## Surface & Texture
Quiet matte stone planes with subtle inner hairline framing.

## Motion & Interaction
280ms measured classical ease.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons use uppercase tracked labels with crisp borders; cards use balanced symmetrical padding.

## What to Avoid
Neon glows, bouncy cartoon physics, or heavy rounded pills.
