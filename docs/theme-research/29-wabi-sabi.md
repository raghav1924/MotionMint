# Wabi Sabi — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://www.siteinspire.com/websites, https://www.pinterest.com/

## Observed Design Principles
Organic asymmetry, muted natural earth & ceramic tones, subtle kintsugi gold repair accents, and calm imperfection.

## Light Variant Specification
Raw washi `#F5F2EB` canvas, `#FBF9F4` ceramic surface, `#2B2823` sumi ink, `#8C6D46` kintsugi bronze + `#6B7B5C` moss accents.

## Dark Variant Specification
Charred cedar `#141311` canvas, `#1E1C19` stoneware surface, `#EAE5DC` washi ink, `#C8A97E` kintsugi gold + `#8FA37E` moss accents.

## Typography
Calm editorial serif + quiet sans, generous line-height.

## Colour
Washi, stoneware, sumi ink, moss, and subtle kintsugi gold.

## Geometry
10px–18px asymmetric organic radii, 1px soft natural borders.

## Surface & Texture
Matte ceramic/washi surface with whisper-soft natural shadow.

## Motion & Interaction
280ms unhurried natural ease.

## Translation to React Components
Cards and buttons feel handcrafted, calm, and organic without sterile symmetry.

## What to Avoid
Neon cyberpunk glows or aggressive sharp-edged brutalism.
