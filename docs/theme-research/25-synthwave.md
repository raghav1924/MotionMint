# Synthwave / Retrowave — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/synthwave-ui, https://www.behance.net/

## Observed Design Principles
80s outrun sunset gradients (purple-to-pink-to-amber), neon laser highlights, and perspective horizon grids.

## Light Variant Specification
Sunset vapor `#FDF2F8` canvas, `#FFFFFF` card, `#2E1065` deep violet ink, `#DB2777` + `#7C3AED` neon retrowave accents.

## Dark Variant Specification
Outrun night `#0E051E` canvas, `#190B33` surface, `#FDF4FF` ink, `#FF2A85` hot pink + `#00E5FF` laser cyan accents.

## Typography
Bold geometric sans + italic/mono 80s accents.

## Colour
Deep violet, hot pink, laser cyan, and sunset amber.

## Geometry
8px–12px sleek retro corners, 1.5px neon-tinted borders.

## Surface & Texture
Gradient sheen and subtle horizon gridlines.

## Motion & Interaction
200ms smooth synth glide.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons glow with pink/cyan outrun highlights; cards frame gradients cleanly.

## What to Avoid
Muted grayscale or raw concrete textures.
