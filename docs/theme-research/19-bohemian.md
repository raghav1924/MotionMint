# Bohemian — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://www.pinterest.com/, https://www.behance.net/

## Observed Design Principles
Warm sun-washed earth tones, organic curved geometry, botanical terracotta/olive accents, and natural tactile warmth.

## Light Variant Specification
Sun-baked sand `#FBF6EE` canvas, `#FFFDF9` surface, `#2C221E` espresso ink, `#C2410C` terracotta + `#65A30D` olive accents.

## Dark Variant Specification
Deep clay `#1A1412` canvas, `#261E1A` warm surface, `#F5ECE2` sand ink, `#FB923C` terracotta + `#A3E635` sage accents.

## Typography
Warm humanistic serif + organic sans.

## Colour
Terracotta, sand, olive, ochre, and espresso.

## Geometry
16px–24px soft organic curves.

## Surface & Texture
Warm matte paper/linen feel with soft natural shadows.

## Motion & Interaction
240ms warm organic ease.

## Translation to React Components
Buttons feel warm and rounded; cards use earthy borders and soft sand surfaces.

## What to Avoid
Cold clinical `#0000FF` or harsh metallic chrome.
