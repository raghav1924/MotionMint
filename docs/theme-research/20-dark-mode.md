# Dark Mode UI — Design Research

- **Researched:** 2026-11-25
- **References:** https://dribbble.com/search/dark-mode-ui, https://linear.app/

## Observed Design Principles
Component-level dark precision: layered obsidian surfaces, crisp 1px hairline separation, and controlled luminous indigo/mint accents.

## Light Variant Specification
Crisp daylight slate `#F1F5F9` interpretation of the precision dark-UI grammar (`#FFFFFF` cards, `#0F172A` ink, `#6366F1` focus).

## Dark Variant Specification
Deep obsidian `#09090B` canvas, `#111114` surface, `#18181C` elevated, `#FAFAFA` ink, `#10B981` + `#38BDF8` luminous accents.

## Typography
Inter / Geist geometric sans + JetBrains Mono.

## Colour
Layered obsidian neutrals with high-contrast text and restrained accent.

## Geometry
12px–14px precision radii, 1px hairline borders.

## Surface & Texture
Subtle elevation contrast with crisp 1px edge definition.

## Motion & Interaction
160ms precision ease.

## Translation to React Components
Controls mirror high-craft developer tools with sharp focus rings and tabular numbers.

## What to Avoid
Muddy low-contrast gray-on-gray text.
